## 🛠️ How It Works

AgentDisplay uses a few clever technologies to keep you informed:

- **WebSocket Connection** – This keeps a real-time link between your computer and the display. Whenever something changes, the screen updates instantly.
- **ESP-IDF Framework** – A powerful system that makes the hardware work smoothly
- **FastAPI Dashboard** – A simple web page that runs on your computer, giving you control and visibility
- **PDM Voice Technology** – The built-in microphone picks up your voice, processes it with speech recognition, and responds with spoken answers

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

If you run into problems, try these quick fixes:

- **Display not showing anything** – Check the USB connection and ensure your device is powered on
- **No sound from voice responses** – Make sure the speaker is connected and not muted on your device
- **Wi-Fi not connecting** – Move your ESP32-S3 closer to your router and try again
- **Dashboard not loading** – Close and reopen the dashboard application on your computer

Most issues have simple solutions, and your display will be back to normal in no time.
